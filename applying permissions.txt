permissions:
  resource-based - scope-based
  
scoped-based você aplica no escopo ou nos resources

resource-based você aplica nos resources

scopes representam conjunto de direitos num resource

In order to grant/deny access to a resource:
  1.
  - Define your policies
  - Define your permissions
  - Apply your policies to your permissions
  - Associate your permissions to a scope or resource
  2.
  - Group policies under an aggregated policy
  - Associate the aggregated policy with scope-based permission.
  - Apply scoped-base permission to the resource
  - Apply scoped-base permission to all associated scope.
  3.
  - Create permissions solely for your resources via resource-based permissions.
  - Associate other permissions solely with a scope via scoped-based permission.
